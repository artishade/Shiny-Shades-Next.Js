the atelier · since 2023

Cut in Dhaka,
worn everywhere

Shiny Shades began in a two-room workshop in Mohammadpur with one belief: a woman should never have to choose between her grandmother’s grace and her own generation’s swagger.

Our karigars hand-finish every piece — French seams on satin, real zari on Banarasi silk, sequins sewn one bead at a time. Western cuts hang beside heritage weaves because most of us are both, and our racks should say so.

“Designed with love for the modern woman” — it’s stitched into the label. Literally.
